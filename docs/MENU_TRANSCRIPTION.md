## How it was transcribed

- Every line is kept as printed, including the sheet's own spellings
  ("Lemmon", "Banans", "Ruben", "Cordon Blue", "Marsh mellows"). Fix them in the app if you want.
- Each item keeps its printed role: unlabeled entrée = **Main**, `A:` = **Alternate**,
  `D:` = **Dessert**, `F:` = **Fruit**, `S:` = **Salad**, soups and sides marked as such.
- The standing breakfast line "A: Scrambled Eggs / Danish-Doughnut-Yogurt" is recorded on every day.
- Nothing unreadable was filled in by guesswork. Uncertain lines carry a ⚠ review note.
