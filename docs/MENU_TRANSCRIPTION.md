# Menu transcription — Fall/Winter, Weeks 1–4

Source: four photographs of the posted Fall/Winter sheets (IMG_2229–IMG_2232).
Data file: `src/lib/menu/fallWinter.ts`.

## How it was transcribed

- Every line is kept as printed, including the sheet's own spellings
  ("Lemmon", "Banans", "Ruben", "Cordon Blue", "Marsh mellows"). Fix them in the app if you want.
- Each item keeps its printed role: unlabeled entrée = **Main**, `A:` = **Alternate**,
  `D:` = **Dessert**, `F:` = **Fruit**, `S:` = **Salad**, soups and sides marked as such.
- The standing breakfast line "A: Scrambled Eggs / Danish-Doughnut-Yogurt" is recorded on every day.
- Nothing unreadable was filled in by guesswork. Uncertain lines carry a ⚠ review note.

## Rotation anchor

The rotation needs a real, facility-confirmed date for **Week 1, Sunday**. The demo starts with
**Sunday, November 8, 2026**, worked out from the photographed supper slip dated *Monday 16th*
(Vegetable Frittata / Chicken Pot Pie = Week 2 Monday dinner). It is marked **unconfirmed**, and the
app shows a banner until someone confirms it under **Menu → Rotation start date**.
